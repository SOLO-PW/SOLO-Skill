## 识别原则

### 核心原则

1. **整体风格优先** - 先判断作品风格和画风
2. **标志性元素匹配** - 识别独特的视觉标志
3. **色彩方案关联** - 特定IP有标志性的配色
4. **不确定时描述** - 无法确定具体IP时，描述风格特征

### 识别置信度

| 置信度 | 判断标准 | 输出方式 |
|--------|----------|----------|
| 高 | 标志性角色+画风+配色全部匹配 | 直接说出IP名称 |
| 中 | 风格相似，部分特征匹配 | "疑似XX风格" |
| 低 | 仅有风格相似 | 描述风格特征，不猜测IP |

## IP提示词模板

### 游戏IP风格模板

**原神风格:**
```
Genshin Impact style, [角色描述], [元素主题], [服装细节], anime style, vibrant colors, detailed character design, fantasy RPG

示例:
Genshin Impact style, young female character with long blue hair and horn accessories, ice element theme, elegant blue and white dress with fur trim, anime style, vibrant colors, detailed character design, fantasy RPG
```

**宝可梦风格:**
```
Pokemon style, [生物/角色描述], [类型], [环境], cute anime style, colorful, simple design

示例:
Pokemon style, cute electric mouse creature with red cheeks and yellow fur, electric type, grass field background, cute anime style, colorful, simple design
```

### 动漫IP风格模板

**吉卜力风格:**
```
Studio Ghibli style, [场景描述], [角色描述], [氛围], hand-drawn animation, soft colors, detailed background, nostalgic atmosphere

示例:
Studio Ghibli style, young girl with short hair flying on a broom over a coastal town, hand-drawn animation, soft colors, detailed background, nostalgic atmosphere
```

**新海诚风格:**
```
Makoto Shinkai style, [场景描述], [光照效果], [氛围], realistic background, vivid colors, light rays, dramatic sky

示例:
Makoto Shinkai style, two teenagers standing on a rooftop looking at the sunset over Tokyo skyline, realistic background, vivid colors, light rays through clouds, dramatic sky
```

### 影视IP风格模板

**漫威风格:**
```
Marvel cinematic style, [角色描述], [动作], [环境], realistic, dramatic lighting, epic composition, superhero aesthetic

示例:
Marvel cinematic style, armored superhero in red and gold suit flying above city skyline, repulsor beams glowing, realistic, dramatic lighting, epic composition, superhero aesthetic
```

**迪士尼风格:**
```
Disney/Pixar style, [角色描述], [场景], [氛围], 3D animation, rounded designs, expressive eyes, magical atmosphere

示例:
Disney/Pixar style, young princess with long golden hair in magical forest, surrounded by friendly animals, 3D animation, rounded designs, expressive eyes, magical atmosphere
```

### 品牌元素提示词

当图片包含品牌元素时，可以这样描述：

```
[品牌名] [产品类型], [产品特征], [使用场景], [风格]

示例:
Apple iPhone 15 Pro, titanium frame, triple camera system, held in hand, modern minimalist style

Louis Vuitton Neverfull bag, monogram canvas, leather handles, carried by woman, luxury fashion photography
```

## IP识别注意事项

### 风格相似但非原IP

当图片风格类似某IP但不是原IP时：

1. **描述风格参考**: "类似XX风格"
2. **说明差异**: 指出与原IP的不同
3. **提供替代建议**: 推荐相似风格的关键词

### 融合风格

当图片融合多种IP风格时：

1. **识别主导风格**: 确定主要参考的IP
2. **描述融合元素**: 说明哪些元素来自哪个IP
3. **提供混合提示词**: 结合多个风格关键词

### 原创设计

当图片是原创设计时：

1. **不要强行关联IP**
2. **描述设计风格**: 提取风格特征
3. **提供风格参考**: 推荐相似风格的关键词
